# Changelog

> Following [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/)
> and [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-05

First release, extracted from the signify-rs project with its history.

### Added

- CESR v1 primitives: Base64 and byte helpers, derivation code tables, qualified and indexed
  material, KERI v1 counters, numbers, sequence numbers, Base64 text and SAD paths.
- The `crypto` feature, off by default: BLAKE3 digests, Ed25519 and P-256 verification, Ed25519
  signing, Argon2id salt derivation, and X25519 sealed-box encryption of seeds and salts.
- Cross-language fixtures generated by [signify-ts], with generators pinned through npm.

[signify-ts]: https://github.com/WebOfTrust/signify-ts
